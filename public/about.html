01 — Mission

Why we exist.

Switch was founded on one idea: Roblox is the most underestimated creative platform on the planet. The audience is there. The tools are there. What's been missing is studios that treat the craft with the same rigour as the biggest names in the industry.

We're building those games. Titles that earn their player counts. Communities that outlast the hype cycle. Experiences that players come back to — not because they have to, but because they want to.

02 — Values

What we stand for.

001

Craft Over Speed

We'd rather ship one exceptional game than three mediocre ones. Quality is non-negotiable.

002

Community First

Every decision runs through one filter: does this make the experience better for the players?

003

Long-Term Thinking

We build for the long run — making commitments to players we intend to honour for years.

004

Trust & Transparency

No spin. When we make a mistake, we say so — and fix it.

005

Relentless Iteration

Launch is day one. We watch the data, listen to players, and update constantly.

006

Talent Without Ego

The best idea wins — regardless of who had it. No hierarchy, just great work.

03 — History

How we got here.

March 2025 — Founded

Switch is born

The studio is founded with one goal: build Roblox games that actually feel like games. Starting with nothing but ideas and a vision for what quality on the platform could look like.

August 2025 — First Title

Tsubasa Soccer enters development

Work begins on our first title. Months of prototyping, playtesting, and relentless iteration to get the feel right before anything ships.

7 March 2026 — Launch

Tsubasa Soccer launches

Tsubasa Soccer goes live on Roblox. The response confirms what we believed from day one — there's a real appetite for higher-quality experiences on the platform.

2026 — Now

Still building

The mission hasn't changed. We're still a team who love games and believe Roblox is one of the most exciting creative platforms in the world. The best chapters are ahead.
