01 — Mission

Play is
serious
business.

We believe that great games change people. They create friendships across continents, teach skills no classroom can, and give players a place to feel genuinely alive. That's why we treat every game we build as a long-term commitment — not a quick launch.

Switch was founded on one idea: Roblox is the most underestimated creative platform on the planet. The audience is there. The tools are there. What's been missing is studios that treat the craft with the same rigour as the biggest names in the industry.

We're building those games. Titles that earn their player counts. Communities that outlast the hype cycle. Experiences that players come back to — not because they have to, but because they want to.

02 — Values

What we stand for.

001

Craft Over Speed

We'd rather ship one game that's truly exceptional than three that are merely good. Quality is our competitive advantage — and we protect it fiercely.

002

Community First

Our players aren't a metric. They're the reason we exist. Every design decision runs through one filter: does this make the experience better for the people playing it?

003

Long-Term Thinking

We build for the long run. That means saying no to shortcuts, investing in our team, and making commitments to players that we intend to honour for years.

004

Trust & Transparency

We communicate openly with our players and our team. No spin. No vague roadmaps. When we make a mistake, we say so — and we fix it.

005

Relentless Iteration

Launch is day one, not the finish line. We watch the data, listen to feedback, and update constantly — because a great game is never truly finished.

006

Talent Without Ego

The best idea wins — regardless of who had it. We hire exceptional people and trust them to do exceptional work, without layers of hierarchy getting in the way.

03 — History

How we got here.

2022 — FOUNDED

Switch is born

A small group of passionate Roblox developers come together with a shared vision: build games that actually feel like games. The studio starts with nothing but ideas and determination.

2023 — FIRST LAUNCH

Our first title ships

After months of prototyping and playtesting, Switch launches its first public game. The response from the community confirms that players are hungry for higher-quality Roblox experiences.

2024 — GROWTH

The team scales

With growing player counts and a reputation for quality, Switch begins growing the team — bringing in talented developers, designers and community managers to take things to the next level.

2025 — EXPANSION

Multi-game studio

Switch expands its portfolio, running multiple live games simultaneously. We invest in internal tools, player-facing systems, and a culture that can support long-term, sustainable growth.

2026 — NOW

Still building

The mission hasn't changed. We're still a team of people who love games and believe Roblox is one of the most exciting creative platforms in the world. The best chapters are ahead.
